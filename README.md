### Key Features of this Website
1. **Interactive Research Search Side (`/` or Ctrl/Cmd+K):**
   - Instant fuzzy search across all research topics, mathematical formulas (Magnus-Tetens, VPD), models (YOLOv11, Bi-LSTM, Qwen SLM), chemical safety interlock rules, Guide Dr. Anbazhagan M., and Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham credentials.
2. **Prominent Academic Supervision & Institutional Leadership Section:**
   - Dedicated profile for Guide Dr. Anbazhagan M., Thesis Advisor & Research Supervisor.
   - Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham emblems, accreditation (NAAC A++, NIRF #7), Chancellor Satguru Sri Mata Amritanandamayi Devi, Principal Dr. Vidhya Balasubramanian, and HOD Dr. P. Bagavathi Sivakumar.
   - Complete reproduction of the official Department of CSE Bonafide Certificate.
3. **The 5 Core Implementation Innovations:**
   - **Pillar 01:** Hardware-Free Thermodynamic Twin (Open-Meteo, Magnus-Tetens, VPD).
   - **Pillar 02:** Pre-Symptomatic Bi-LSTM Sequence Forecaster (24h/48h/72h ahead, ROC-AUC 0.854).
   - **Pillar 03:** Dual-Engine Vision Suite (Ultralytics YOLOv11m 99.74% & EfficientNet-B0 99.45%).
   - **Pillar 04:** Phenology-Gated Safety Interlock (Honeybee protection & Harvest PHI lockout).
   - **Pillar 05:** Offline Edge SLM "Dr. Thangavelu" (Qwen2.5-3B QLoRA, ChromaDB RAG, English & Tamil).
4. **Live Interactive Thermodynamic Digital Twin & What-If Studio:**
   - Modulate ambient temperature, humidity, and solar radiation with real-time sliders.
   - Computes indoor temperature, relative humidity, $E_s$, $E_a$, $VPD$, and dew point in software ($0 hardware sensor cost).
   - Closed-loop actuator simulation: Roof Vents, Exhaust Fans, Foggers, and Shading Screens with 4-organ vegetative stress telemetry.
5. **High-Performance Compute & Artifact Deployment Pipeline:**
   - NVIDIA H200 NVL (150GB VRAM) training convergence metrics and llama.cpp Q4_K_M 1.93 GB quantization.
6. **Thesis Chapters & Indian Patent Documentation:**
   - Breakdown of all 8 thesis chapters (162 pages).
   - Patent application claims filed through Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham with the Indian Patent Office.
   - One-click copyable BibTeX citation generator.
